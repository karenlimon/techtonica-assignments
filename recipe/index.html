Café de Olla (Mexican Coffee)

Coffee has always been a part of my life. As a little girl, I would sit at the dinner table with my mother and grandmother while they sipped their coffee in the evening, debriefing after their long days. Today, making my morning coffee has become my version of my daily ritual and one of the ways I love to show love to the beautiful people in my life. I'm so excited to share this traditional spiced version with you! It's infused with warm, cozy flavors like cinnamon, star anise, and clove. Whether you like your coffee hot or poured over ice, this recipe is perfect for anyone craving a comforting, flavorful cup.

  • 8-10 cups water (the less water you have, the stronger it will be)
  • 1 80z piloncillo
  • 2-3 cinnamon sticks
  • 1 star anise
  • 3-4 cloves
  • 1 teaspoon of vanilla extract (or paste)
  • 1 cupe of ground coffee
